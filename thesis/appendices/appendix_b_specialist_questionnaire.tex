\chapter{Specialist Questionnaire and Interview Guide}
\label{app:specialist_q}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Appendix B: Specialist Questionnaire and Interview Guide}

\noindent
This appendix reproduces the structured questionnaire and the
semi-structured interview guide administered to the 50-respondent
specialist sample between March and May 2025
(Section~\ref{sec:instruments} of Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}).

\section*{Part I: Structured Questionnaire}

\subsection*{Background}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Role: $\square$~Producer \quad $\square$~Director \quad
        $\square$~OTT acquisition / commissioning head \quad
        $\square$~Distributor \quad $\square$~Exhibitor \quad
        $\square$~Marketing / PR \quad $\square$~Music-label exec \quad
        $\square$~Trade analyst \quad $\square$~Other
        \rule{2cm}{0.4pt}
  \item Years of professional experience in Tamil cinema:
        $\square$~$<$5 \quad $\square$~5--10 \quad $\square$~10--20
        \quad $\square$~$>$20.
  \item Number of Tamil films you have been involved in (any
        capacity) since 2019: \rule{2cm}{0.4pt}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Production-Budget Brackets}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Of the films you were involved in 2018--2019, what \% fell in
        each bracket: $<$5 cr; 5--15 cr; 15--30 cr; 30--60 cr;
        $>$60 cr.
  \item Repeat for films in 2023--2024.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Theatrical-to-OTT Window}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Of the Tamil films released in 2018--2019, what \% had a
        window in each band: same-day OTT; 1--2 wks; 3--4 wks;
        5--8 wks; $>$8 wks.
  \item Repeat for Tamil films released in 2023--2024.
  \item Median window for the average mid-budget release today
        (in days): \rule{2cm}{0.4pt}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{OTT Acquisition-Price Index}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Indexed against 2018 baseline (=100), the OTT acquisition
        price for a star-led Tamil film was approximately:
        2019 \rule{1cm}{0.4pt}; 2020 \rule{1cm}{0.4pt};
        2021 \rule{1cm}{0.4pt}; 2022 \rule{1cm}{0.4pt};
        2023 \rule{1cm}{0.4pt}; 2024 \rule{1cm}{0.4pt};
        2025 \rule{1cm}{0.4pt}.
  \item Repeat for mid-budget films.
  \item Repeat for small / indie films.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Star-Fee Restructuring}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Median \% adjustment in star fees 2019 $\to$ 2024 by tier:
        top-tier \rule{1cm}{0.4pt}; A-list \rule{1cm}{0.4pt};
        B-list \rule{1cm}{0.4pt}; character / supporting
        \rule{1cm}{0.4pt}.
  \item Has the bargaining-power balance between star and
        producer / OTT shifted? Open comment.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Marketing-Channel Mix}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
  \item For the films you marketed, indicate \% of releases that
        used each channel \emph{in 2018--2019} and \emph{in
        2023--2024}: social-media campaign; influencer marketing;
        OTT in-app promotion; YouTube digital premiere; audio-launch
        live-stream; theatrical hoardings; print advertising; radio.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Revenue Mix and Streams}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Indicate \% revenue mix for an average Tamil release
        \emph{in 2018--2019}: theatrical; OTT; satellite; music /
        digital; international.
  \item Repeat for \emph{2023--2024}.
  \item Median perceived change (\%) from 2019 to 2024 in each of:
        theatrical revenue per film; OTT licensing revenue;
        satellite TV revenue; music / digital rights; international;
        in-cinema F\&B.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Self-Reported Outlook (Likert 1--5)}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
  \item The hybrid 3--4 week theatre-OTT window will become the
        permanent norm. (1--5)
  \item Star-fee inflation will continue to correct over the next
        2--3 years. (1--5)
  \item Mid-budget content-led films are now the safest commercial
        bet. (1--5)
  \item Direct-to-OTT will grow to $>$ 50\% of releases by 2027.
        (1--5)
  \item Pan-India ambition is now an essential part of project
        greenlighting. (1--5)
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part II: Semi-Structured Interview Guide (45--90 minutes)}

\subsection*{Cluster 1 -- Pandemic operating decisions}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
\item Walk me through how your operating decisions changed in
      March--June 2020.
\item Which of those decisions have proved permanent vs transient?
\item What did you most under-estimate during the lockdown phase?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Cluster 2 -- Demand sense-making}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
\item How are you reading the post-pandemic Tamil viewer?
\item What are the demand signals you trust, and which do you
      discount?
\item How is the star-vs-content question playing out in your slate?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Cluster 3 -- Capital flows}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
\item How have financiers shifted their risk appetite?
\item How has the OTT-acquisition negotiation changed?
\item Where do you expect investment to flow in the next 3 years?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Cluster 4 -- Labour-market dynamics}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
\item Has the technician / crew labour market re-formed since 2020?
\item How are stars and their teams behaving differently?
\item What hiring or contracting changes have you made?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Cluster 5 -- 3--5 year outlook}
\begin{enumerate}[resume,itemsep=2pt]
\item Where do you see Tamil cinema in 2027--2028?
\item What is the biggest risk you see for the industry?
\item What is the biggest opportunity?
\end{enumerate}
